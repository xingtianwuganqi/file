Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use PawTime Application (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

1. Device information (such as device model and operating system).

2. Usage information (such as app features used and interaction logs).

3. Information you provide, including pet records, notes, photos, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

1. Provide and improve pet record and management features.

2. Enhance user experience and app performance.

3. Maintain security and stability of the App.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Your data is stored only for as long as necessary to provide the services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights.

5. Cloud and Sync Services

If you enable cloud backup or synchronization features, your data may be stored on secure third-party cloud services.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your information.

You may manage your data through the App or by contacting us.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children. If you are under the age required by your local laws, please use the App with parental guidance.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App.

This Privacy Policy is effective as of the date of publication.
