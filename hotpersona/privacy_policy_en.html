HotPersona Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

1. Information We Process

HotPersona may process account information such as email address, login identifiers, authentication tokens, selected personas, selected focus options, perspective styles, input text, generated posts, generation history, feedback content, contact information submitted with feedback, subscription status, purchase verification data, and basic device or usage information needed to operate the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, provide topic feeds, generate and store posts, sync selected personas and styles, process subscriptions and entitlements, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, improve reliability, respond to feedback, and maintain account security features.

3. AI Inputs And Generated Posts

Text you enter, selected persona settings, hot topic context, and generated outputs may be processed to provide generation features and history. Avoid entering sensitive personal, financial, medical, confidential, or third-party proprietary information into prompts or custom persona settings.

4. Payments

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled through the applicable platform store. HotPersona may receive purchase identifiers, transaction information, receipt data, product identifiers, and entitlement status to verify Pro access. Payment card details are handled by the platform store, not by HotPersona.

5. Storage And Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No system is perfectly secure, and transmission or storage of information always carries some risk. You are responsible for protecting your device and account access.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate authentication, hosting, analytics, support, AI generation, payment verification, and security functions, or when required by law.

7. Your Choices

You can choose what content to enter, whether to use logged-in features, and whether to purchase Pro access. Account security tools may allow account deletion or related account actions. For data or privacy requests, contact us using the email below.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or account requests, contact us at rxswiftc@gmail.com.
