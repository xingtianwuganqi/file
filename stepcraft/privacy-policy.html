1. Content processed on your device

StepCraft is a local video-to-tutorial tool provided by the developer identified on its App Store listing. It does not require a StepCraft account and does not operate a developer server for uploading or processing your media.

Videos you select are copied into the App’s local project storage. Selected frames, requested and actual frame times, project titles, step text, crops, annotations, template settings and draft metadata are processed on your device. The App does not automatically send this content to us or to a cloud processing service.

2. Local preferences, caches and retention

If you save a common style, StepCraft stores its template, theme and aspect ratio locally and applies it to new projects. It does not copy your content into that preference or change existing projects. You can replace or clear the preference from the template preview.

Thumbnails, preview data and intermediate export files support editing and generation. Temporary files may be cleared when no active operation or sharing session needs them. Project content remains locally until you delete it, remove the App or otherwise remove the data through device controls.

Imported project media copies are configured to be excluded from system backup. Other local metadata or preferences may be included in Apple backup or device-transfer features depending on system settings. StepCraft provides no independent cloud backup or account-based draft restoration.

3. Photos access and permissions

Import uses Apple’s system picker, through which you choose a single video. StepCraft does not need general access to your entire photo library merely to let you choose that item. A video stored in iCloud may first be downloaded by the system under your Apple settings.

Saving an exported creation requests permission to add items to Photos when you choose that action. You can change permissions in Settings. If permission is denied, you can continue editing and use available alternatives, such as system sharing to Files.

StepCraft does not request camera, microphone, location or contact-book access for its core tutorial workflow. It does not include the source video’s audio or a voice recording in step-card video exports.

4. Purchases and Apple services

Apple and StoreKit handle Pro purchases and restoration. StepCraft checks verified transaction and entitlement information on the device, such as the product identifier and purchase, approval or revocation status, to decide which Pro features are available.

We do not receive your card number or full payment-account details through the App, and we do not maintain a developer purchase-account database. Apple processes transactions, purchase history and related system information under its own policies.

StoreKit and App Store services may require network access even though media editing is local. Restore Purchases can initiate an Apple account synchronization only when you actively request restoration from the purchase page.

5. Information you choose to share

Saving to Photos places files in your library, where your system photo-sync and backup settings may apply. System sharing sends the files you choose to the app, service or recipient you select. Those destinations may process or store the content under their own policies. Deleting a local draft cannot retract an already shared file.

Feedback does not automatically attach original media, drafts or device logs. If you email us or contact us on X, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your address or account name, message and attachments. Do not send sensitive or third-party information unless it is necessary and you are entitled to do so.

6. Support correspondence

We use correspondence to understand and respond to your request, troubleshoot issues and, when necessary, meet legal obligations. Support information is handled through the email or social service you choose. These providers may process information in other countries under their own terms and privacy policies.

We keep support correspondence only as reasonably necessary for those purposes and any applicable legal requirements. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification. There is no StepCraft account to delete, and we cannot remotely view or erase local drafts on your device.

7. Analytics, advertising and diagnostics

The shipping App has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, tracking pixels or default content or usage telemetry sent to the developer. We do not sell your personal information or use your tutorial content for advertising or model training.

Apple may make aggregated App Store information, purchase reports or system crash and diagnostic reports available to developers according to its services and your settings. This is separate from an in-app analytics service operated by StepCraft. Avoid including private content in any diagnostic material you choose to send us.

8. Bundled resources, local documents and external links

Background music, labelled example frames, licenses and these legal documents are bundled with the App. Viewing these documents, displaying examples and exporting with bundled music do not require downloading a remote page or track.

The legal-document viewer disables page scripts and uses a non-persistent web data store. The bundled pages contain no analytics, forms, remote fonts or embedded remote media. They do not use tracking cookies.

External links, including contact, music-source, license and Apple links, open only when you choose them. The browser, mail client or destination service may then receive ordinary connection information and process data under its own policies. This Policy does not govern those services.

9. Deleting and controlling your data

You can delete an individual project or all projects in the App and clear temporary cache. Project deletion removes the corresponding in-app media and draft data. Common-style preferences can be cleared separately in the template preview. Removing the App normally removes its local data, subject to the operating system’s behavior and any backups or transfers.

Manage exported copies in Photos, Files, recipient apps and services separately. Pro purchase records are managed by Apple and are not erased by deleting a local project. Manage Apple account, purchase, privacy and device-backup settings through Apple’s controls.

10. Security, children and your rights

Local processing reduces the need to transmit media, but no storage or system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device, keep software updated and retain copies of important work. StepCraft cannot recover media that was never sent to us.

The App does not provide a children’s account system or knowingly seek children’s personal information. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the request under applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information held about you, including rights to access, correction, deletion, objection or restriction and a complaint to an appropriate authority. For information held by us through support correspondence, contact us below. For local content or Apple-held records, the device and provider controls described above are the relevant starting points.

11. Updates and contact

This Policy reflects the App’s current practices and may be updated alongside changes to the App or applicable requirements. The date at the top identifies this version. Material changes requiring notice or consent will be handled as required by applicable law.
