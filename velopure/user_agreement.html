1. The Service

VeloPure is a cycling utility that can record rides, calculate cycling metrics, display map routes, save local ride history, create route videos, show Live Activities, write eligible workouts to Apple Health, and synchronize completed rides from a paired Apple Watch to an iPhone.

The App does not currently provide user accounts, developer-operated cloud storage, social networking, coaching, emergency response, or medical services.
