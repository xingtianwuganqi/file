The product name, localized price, currency, and available functionality are displayed in the App before purchase. Apple’s purchase confirmation sheet shows the final transaction information. You will not be charged unless you authenticate and confirm the purchase through Apple.

3. Fourteen-Day Trial

The trial begins when you confirm the zero-price “14-day Trial” non-consumable In-App Purchase through Apple and lasts for fourteen consecutive 24-hour periods. No payment is required to begin the trial, the trial does not automatically convert into a purchase, and you will not be charged when it ends.

Trial eligibility and timing are determined using Apple-verified transaction information. Reinstalling the App, changing the device date, or restoring a backup does not create a new trial. After the trial ends, continued access to the App’s ride features requires a verified VeloPure Pro purchase. A ride already in progress may be completed before access is restricted.

After Apple verifies a valid, non-revoked VeloPure Pro transaction, the App marks Pro as unlocked for the Apple Account associated with that transaction. “One-time” means there is no recurring billing for the current non-consumable product.
